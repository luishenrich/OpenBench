# Why trust the judge

On the first judged support run, a person agreed with the jury on 8 of 10 rubric cases (Spearman 0.68). Where both Luna and DeepSeek scored the same answer, they disagreed, and those rows were scored before the judge was shown the system prompt. We publish that disagreement and we do not treat the judge as the label until a person has looked.
